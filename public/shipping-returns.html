FULFILMENT POLICY · UK DISPATCH

Shipping & Returns Information

All Penbrae & Co Ltd orders are inspected, packed in UV-opaque archival vessels, and dispatched from Cardiff, Wales.

I. UK Dispatch & Postage Tariffs

Orders placed before 14:00 GMT Monday to Friday are prepared for dispatch within 24 hours via Royal Mail Tracked services:

  • UK Tracked 48 Service: £4.50 flat rate (2–3 working days).
  • Complimentary UK Dispatch: Automatically applied on all orders of £50.00 or more.

II. Packaging & Freshness Preservation

Single-harvest orthodox teas, high-curcumin rhizomes, and smoked chillies are sensitive to ultraviolet light and ambient humidity. Every lot is sealed in airtight tinplate caddies or UV-protected glass jars before leaving our Cardiff room.

III. Returns & Perishable Food Regulations

Under UK Consumer Contracts Regulations, sealed food and beverage items may be returned within 14 days of receipt provided the tamper-evident vessel seal remains unbroken. If any vessel arrives damaged in transit, please contact our Cardiff desk within 7 days for an immediate replacement or full GBP refund.
