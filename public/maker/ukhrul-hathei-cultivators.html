Craft Process
High-ridge mineral shale cultivation and bright autumn ridge sun-drying

Across Sirarakhong Village, Ukhrul District, Manipur, smallholder stewards maintain cultivation and post-harvest curing traditions calibrated to micro-altitude and seasonal rainfall. Rather than blending harvests into anonymous regional bulk, each lot is kept distinct by harvest week and curing loft.

When temperatures shift after the monsoon, slow maturation concentrates essential oils, natural sugars, and mineral character—yielding ingredients with unmistakable sense of place.

“When the harvest is cured slowly on woven bamboo, the cell wall yields gently. You taste the mist, the soil, and the woodsmoke—never a factory floor.”
