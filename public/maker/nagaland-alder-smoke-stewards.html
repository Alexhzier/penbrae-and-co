PRODUCER PROFILE M-03 · CHILLI STALL

Mon District Alder-Smoke Chilli Stewards

Jhum-cultivated Raja Mircha slow-cured for fourteen days on bamboo racks above smouldering alder-wood hearths.

REGION: Mon & Kohima Uplands, Nagaland
COORDINATES: 26°43′N 95°01′E · 1,460m ASL

PRODUCER DETAILS
Location
Mon & Kohima Uplands, Nagaland
Coordinates

Craft Process
Rotational alder agroforestry and 14-day hearth-loft smoke preservation

Across Mon & Kohima Uplands, Nagaland, smallholder stewards maintain cultivation and post-harvest curing traditions calibrated to micro-altitude and seasonal rainfall. Rather than blending harvests into anonymous regional bulk, each lot is kept distinct by harvest week and curing loft.

When temperatures shift after the monsoon, slow maturation concentrates essential oils, natural sugars, and mineral character—yielding ingredients with an unmistakable sense of place.

“When the harvest is cured slowly on woven bamboo, the cell wall yields gently. You taste the mist, the soil, and the woodsmoke—never a factory floor.”
