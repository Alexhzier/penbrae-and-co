PRODUCER PROFILE M-01 · TEA STALL

Brahmaputra Smallholder Tea Collective

Hand-rolled orthodox tippy Assam & Singpho hearth-smoked bamboo reserve lots cultivated along the southern bank of the Brahmaputra.

REGION: Dibrugarh & Tinsukia, Upper Assam
COORDINATES: 27°28′N 94°54′E · 140m ASL
← Return to Producer Map
PRODUCER DETAILS
Location
Dibrugarh & Tinsukia, Upper Assam
Coordinates
27°28′N 94°54′E · 140m ASL
Craft Process
Ambient bamboo-tray withering, hand-rolling, and controlled oxidation

Across Dibrugarh & Tinsukia, Upper Assam, smallholder stewards maintain cultivation and post-harvest curing traditions calibrated to micro-altitude and seasonal rainfall. Rather than blending harvests into anonymous regional bulk, each lot is kept distinct by harvest week and curing loft.

When temperatures shift after the monsoon, slow maturation concentrates essential oils, natural sugars, and mineral character—yielding ingredients with an unmistakable sense of place.

“When the harvest is cured slowly on woven bamboo, the cell wall yields gently. You taste the mist, the soil, and the woodsmoke—never a factory floor.”
