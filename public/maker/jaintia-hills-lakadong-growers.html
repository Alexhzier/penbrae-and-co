West Jaintia Hills Lakadong Growers

High-curcumin single-origin Lakadong turmeric & sun-cured heirloom hill ginger from the limestone plateaus of Meghalaya.

REGION: Laskein Block, West Jaintia Hills, Meghalaya
COORDINATES: 25°26′N 92°17′E · 1,280m ASL
← Return to Northeast India Atlas
PRODUCER LEDGER
Location
Laskein Block, West Jaintia Hills, Meghalaya
Craft Process
Limestone upland cultivation, spring-water washing, and winter sun-curing

Across Laskein Block, West Jaintia Hills, Meghalaya, smallholder stewards maintain cultivation and post-harvest curing traditions calibrated to micro-altitude and seasonal rainfall. Rather than blending harvests into anonymous regional bulk, each lot is kept distinct by harvest week and curing loft.

When temperatures shift after the monsoon, slow maturation concentrates essential oils, natural sugars, and mineral character—yielding ingredients with unmistakable sense of place.

“When the harvest is cured slowly on woven bamboo, the cell wall yields gently. You taste the mist, the soil, and the woodsmoke—never a factory floor.”
