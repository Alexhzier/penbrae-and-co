COMPANY MONOGRAPH · PAGE 08

From Cardiff to the Hills & Valleys of Northeast India.

Penbrae & Co Ltd is a Cardiff-based company importing rare, handcrafted teas, spices, and chillies from Northeast India—preserving the identity of every region and producer.

I. Who We Are & Why We Started

Penbrae & Co Ltd was established in Cardiff with a clear conviction: that the extraordinary agricultural and culinary heritage of Northeast India deserves to be experienced in its unblended, single-origin form. For generations, harvests from Upper Assam, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Arunachal Pradesh, and Manipur have either remained within local hill markets or been absorbed into anonymous commodity supply chains.

Our purpose is to bridge Cardiff and Northeast India through careful curation and documentary storytelling—allowing cooks, tea drinkers, and curious readers in the United Kingdom to discover not only rare ingredients, but the landscapes and people behind them.

II. Philosophy of Sourcing & Respect for Craftsmanship

True luxury in agriculture lies in patience and specificity. Whether it is smallholder orthodox tea withered on woven bamboo trays in Dibrugarh, high-curcumin Lakadong turmeric cultivated on misty limestone slopes in the West Jaintia Hills, or Raja Mircha chillies cured above smouldering alder hearths in Nagaland, traditional methods cannot be rushed.
