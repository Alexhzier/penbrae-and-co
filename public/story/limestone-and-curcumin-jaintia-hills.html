PRODUCT PROVENANCE MONOGRAPH · PAGE 07

Limestone & Curcumin: Winter Harvest in the West Jaintia Hills

Inside Laskein Block, where mineral-rich limestone uplands and dry January sunshine yield the world’s highest-curcumin heirloom turmeric.

HARVEST CHRONOLOGY
  1. Dawn Mist: Cool valley fog shields the crop from harsh early sun.
  2. Selective Harvest: Hand-gathered at peak volatile oil and sap density.
  3. Bamboo Curing: Slow ambient withering or hearth-loft preservation on woven bamboo.
  4. Cardiff Arrival: Small-batch inspection and sealing in UV-opaque vessels.

Grown without synthetic inputs in the limestone-rich soils of Laskein Block in the West Jaintia Hills. Hand-washed, sliced onto woven bamboo mats, and sun-cured before small-batch stone milling. Across West Jaintia Hills, Meghalaya, the transition from monsoon humidity to crisp post-monsoon nights slows plant metabolism—concentrating aromatics before the harvest window opens.

Tasting profile in the cup or pan: Vibrant saffron-amber pigment, fresh ginger blossom, warm resinous earth, bright citrus peel. Recommended preparation: Bloom 1/2 tsp in warm ghee or cold-pressed oil for 20 seconds before adding liquids, or steep with cracked black pepper.

“When the autumn fog settles over the valley, growth slows to a whisper—concentrating essential oils and mineral character inside every harvest.”
